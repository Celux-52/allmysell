It demands strategic planning, user-centric design, and rigorous testing. Industry data reveals that 75% of apps fail due to a lack of proper initial planning. Here is our proven process at Allmysell for building mobile applications that users love.</p><h2>1. What Happens in Phase 1: Discovery & Strategy? (2-4 Weeks)</h2><p>Before writing a single line of code, we define:</p><ul><li>Target audience and user personas</li><li>Core features for MVP (Minimum Viable Product)</li><li>Technical architecture and technology choices</li><li>Revenue model (subscription, freemium, one-time purchase)</li></ul><h2>2. Why Is Phase 2: UI/UX Design Crucial? (3-6 Weeks)</h2><p>Design is not just aesthetics — it is strategy. Testing high-fidelity interactive prototypes in Figma before development has been shown to catch usability issues that would cost 10x more to fix during coding.</p><h2>3. How Does Phase 3: Development Work? (8-16 Weeks)</h2><p>We use <strong>React Native</strong> for cross-platform development, allowing us to ship both iOS and Android apps from a single codebase.
