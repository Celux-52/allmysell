<p><strong>Hearing the iconic 'Cha-Ching!' sound of your first eBay sale is an incredible feeling. But the real business starts after the payment clears.</strong></p><h2>Introduction</h2><p>The biggest misconception in dropshipping is that the job ends when the item sells. Long-term survival on eBay depends entirely on flawless backend operations. In 2026, eBay's algorithms heavily boost "Top Rated Sellers" who keep buyers happy.</p><h2>Section 1: Order Fulfillment</h2><p>A customer bought your product and paid on eBay. Now, your job is to buy the product from your supplier and ship it directly to the customer.</p><ol><li>Go to the eBay Seller Hub and copy the buyer's full name and address.</li><li>Go to your AliExpress supplier. At checkout, replace your default address with the <strong>customer's address</strong>.</li><li>Always leave a message for the supplier: <em>"I am dropshipping. Please do NOT include any invoices, receipts, QR codes, or promotional materials."</em></li><li>Complete the purchase.</li></ol><h2>Section 2: The Tracking Number Strategy</h2><p>After you place the order, the supplier will ship the item and provide you with a Tracking Number.</p><p><strong>Why Tracking is Critical:</strong> eBay tracks your "Handling Time." If you set a 3-day handling time, you MUST upload that tracking number to eBay within those 3 days. Uploading late increases your "Late Shipment Rate," which tanks your search ranking.</p><h2>Section 3: Customer Service</h2><p>As a dropshipper, you are a brand manager. Reply to customer messages within 12 hours max (ideally under 2 hours). Use templates for common questions like "Where is my package?" Be proactive: send a courtesy message thanking the buyer as soon as you upload the tracking number.</p><h2>Section 4: Handling Returns & Cases</h2><p>You will get "Item Not Received" (INR) or "Item Arrived Broken" messages. It's inevitable.</p><p><strong>INR Cases:</strong> Check the tracking. If it's lost, issue a Full Refund immediately without arguing. The buyer will be happy. You can then open a dispute on AliExpress to get your money back.</p><p><strong>Damaged Returns:</strong> Ask the buyer for a photo of the damage. Send it to your supplier for a refund. On eBay, tell the buyer: "Keep the item, I am issuing a full refund right now." Paying for international return shipping to China costs significantly more than the product itself.</p>
