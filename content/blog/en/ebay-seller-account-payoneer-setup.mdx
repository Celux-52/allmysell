<p><strong>The secret to preventing your new eBay account from being permanently suspended before you even make a sale is in the initial setup.</strong></p><h2>Introduction</h2><p>Under eBay's strict 2026 security policies, nearly 30% of new seller accounts trigger manual reviews in the first week. A proper Payoneer integration ensures your global profits land safely in your local bank account without friction.</p><h2>Section 1: Basic Setup and "Cookie Warming"</h2><p>Creating an account and immediately hitting "Sell" is the fastest way to trigger bots. You must act like an organic user.</p><ol><li><strong>Start as a Normal User:</strong> Create a standard buyer account. Use your real IP address.</li><li><strong>Cookie Warming:</strong> For 2-3 days, browse eBay like a normal customer. Add items to your Watchlist.</li><li><strong>Small Purchase:</strong> Buy a cheap $1-$2 item. This proves you are a real buyer.</li><li><strong>Transition to Seller:</strong> Once your account has aged slightly, click "Sell" to upgrade your profile.</li></ol><h2>Section 2: Payoneer Account Creation & Verification</h2><p>eBay manages global payouts via Payoneer. Go to Payoneer.com and register. If your eBay is Individual, your Payoneer must be Individual. Names must match <strong>exactly</strong>. Upload your Government ID and a Proof of Address in the Verification Center.</p><h2>Section 3: eBay & Payoneer Integration</h2><p>Once Payoneer verifies your documents, log in to the eBay Seller Hub. Follow the prompt to set up "Get paid on eBay." Choose "Existing Payoneer Account" and link the two platforms. eBay will ask for a credit/debit card on file for selling fees.</p><h2>Section 4: Suspension Prevention Guide</h2><ul><li><strong>Name & Address Consistency:</strong> Must be exactly the same across eBay, Payoneer, and your local Bank.</li><li><strong>Consistent IP Usage:</strong> Always log in from the same device and Wi-Fi network.</li><li><strong>Don't Rush:</strong> Your first listing should ideally be a random, used household item.</li><li><strong>Avoid VeRO:</strong> Never list branded items in your first few months.</li></ul>
