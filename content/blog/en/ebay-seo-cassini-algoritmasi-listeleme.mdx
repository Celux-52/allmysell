<p><strong>You might have found the most profitable product in the world, but if eBay's search engine (Cassini) buries it on page 10, you will make exactly zero dollars.</strong></p><h2>Introduction</h2><p>With 1.7 billion active listings daily, standing out on eBay in 2026 is not about luck. It's a game of algorithms and data manipulation. 50% of your dropshipping success comes from sourcing the right product; the other 50% comes from optimizing that product for eBay SEO.</p><h2>Section 1: What is the Cassini Algorithm?</h2><p>Cassini is the core of eBay. Its primary goal is to show products that are <strong>most likely to result in a sale</strong>.</p><ul><li><strong>Click-Through Rate (CTR):</strong> When your item appears in search, how many people click it? (Determined by your main image and title).</li><li><strong>Sell-Through Rate (STR):</strong> Out of 100 people who view your listing, how many actually buy? (Determined by pricing and description).</li><li><strong>Item Specifics Validation:</strong> Cassini heavily favors sellers who meticulously fill out these data points.</li></ul><h2>Section 2: The Art of the Perfect Title</h2><p>The single biggest mistake dropshippers make is copying keyword-stuffed titles directly from AliExpress.</p><p><strong>The Title Formula:</strong> [Main Keyword 1] + [Main Keyword 2] + [Product Function] + [Key Feature] + [Color/Size/Brand (Unbranded)]</p><p>eBay gives you 80 characters. Use at least 75 of them with long-tail keywords found via Zik Analytics. Never use spam words like "WOW" or "HOT SALE."</p><h2>Section 3: Images & Descriptions</h2><p>Never use the low-quality, text-heavy AliExpress stock photos as your main image. Use Canva to create a clean, white background image. Keep your description mobile-friendly: use short paragraphs, bullet points, and clean, legible fonts (67% of eBay traffic is mobile).</p><h2>Section 4: Item Specifics</h2><p>"Item Specifics" are the detailed data points (Brand, Color, Dimensions) below the title. eBay's internal data shows that listings with fully populated Item Specifics receive <strong>up to 40% more visibility</strong>. For generic items, always set Brand to "Unbranded" and UPC to "Does not apply."</p>
