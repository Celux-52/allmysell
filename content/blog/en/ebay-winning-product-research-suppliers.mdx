<p><strong>You cannot make money on eBay by selling the exact same product as everyone else, at a higher price. The secret to scalable income is finding the 'golden products'—high demand, low competition.</strong></p><h2>Introduction</h2><p>E-commerce competition in 2026 is ruthless. Success isn't about guessing; it's about Data-Driven Sourcing—using analytics to put the right product in front of the right buyer.</p><h2>Section 1: Winning Product Criteria</h2><p><strong>The Ideal Product Checklist:</strong></p><ul><li><strong>The Sweet Spot Price:</strong> Between $15 and $50. This is the "impulse buy" range.</li><li><strong>Unbreakable & Lightweight:</strong> Heavy, fragile items eat up your margins in shipping costs.</li><li><strong>Problem-Solvers:</strong> Products that solve a daily annoyance or cater to passionate hobbies.</li><li><strong>High Sell-Through Rate (STR):</strong> At least 50% of the listings should have resulted in a sale over the last 30 days.</li></ul><p><strong>The "Do Not Sell" List:</strong> Electronics, Clothing/Apparel, VeRO/Branded Items, Liquids.</p><h2>Section 2: Data-Driven Sourcing with Zik Analytics</h2><p>Professionals read data. Use Zik Analytics to find winners:</p><ol><li>Dive into evergreen categories like Home & Garden.</li><li>Set the Filters: Price ($15-$50), Listing Type (Fixed Price), Item Location (China/Dropshipped).</li><li><strong>Competitor Sniping:</strong> Find other successful dropshippers. Extract their best-selling items.</li><li>Ensure the product has sold at least 10 times in the last 30 days. Take their winning concept, improve the SEO title, and list it.</li></ol><h2>Section 3: The Art of Supplier Selection</h2><p>Your supplier can make you or break you. AliExpress remains the starting point.</p><p><strong>Anatomy of a Top-Tier Supplier:</strong></p><ul><li><strong>Store Age:</strong> Must be open for at least 2 years.</li><li><strong>Positive Feedback:</strong> Minimum 96% positive feedback rating.</li><li><strong>Trackable Shipping:</strong> ALWAYS select shipping methods that provide a tracking number (e.g., AliExpress Standard Shipping).</li></ul><h2>Section 4: Profit Margins</h2><p>Never list a product unless your projected NET profit margin is at least 20%. Remember to calculate eBay Final Value Fees (13-15% + $0.30) and Payoneer Withdrawal Fees (~2%).</p>
